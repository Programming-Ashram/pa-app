Are you a recent high school graduate, uncertain about your path in the medical field? Fear not, for I am here to guide you towards your dream career.

Embarking on a journey in medicine demands dedication and passion, promising a rewarding and esteemed professional future. If you possess a strong understanding of physics, chemistry, and biology, then a medical college may be the perfect fit for you.

Before delving into preparations, it’s crucial to determine your desired specialization within the vast realm of medical education, whether it be MBBS, BDS, Veterinary Sciences, or others.

For those aspiring to pursue MBBS, clearing the entrance exam is the initial step. There are three primary entrance exams: NEET, AIIMS, and JIPMER.

JIPMER, renowned for its medical programs, conducts its own entrance exam. Preparation must align with their syllabus, as the exam is tailored accordingly. This year, JIPMER’s entrance exam is scheduled for July 2, 2024, with subsequent counseling sessions in June, July, August, and September.

Similarly, AIIMS, a prestigious medical institution, requires clearing its entrance exam held on May 25th and 26th, 2024.

NEET, on the other hand, is the gateway to most medical colleges across India, except AIIMS and JIPMER. With a minimum score of 140 out of 720, aspiring students become eligible for admission. NEET’s exam date is set for May 5, 2024. Admissions to government-funded Deemed Universities are conducted through MCC counseling, while state-wise counseling is arranged for state universities.

In the event of not securing a seat through counseling, direct admission to medical colleges for MBBS is also a viable option.

Undoubtedly, initiating a new endeavor often brings forth questions and uncertainties. However, considering the advantages of pursuing an MBBS degree in India can offer clarity amidst the decision-making process.

2. Advantages Of Pursuing MBBS In India

  • Enrollment in MCI-approved colleges.
  • Admission through NEET entrance test, ensuring quality education.
  • Eligibility to engage with patients during internships.
  • No language barrier.
  • No food-related challenges.
  • Exemption from screening tests for practicing medicine in India.

3. Basic Subject And Skill Required

The basic MBBS course contains the basic subject like Physiology, Biochemistry, Microbiology,
Anatomy, Pharmacology and Pathology.

  • Responsible attitude
  • Patience and Compassion
  • Mental alertness at all time
  • Constant updating of knowledge
  • Ability to work with a team
  • Good Stamina

4. Eligibility

  • The student must have cleared the 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology in the 12th
    stream.
  • A minimum of 50% marks are required for admission to the MBBS degree course.
  • The student's age should be between 17 years to 25 years.

5. Type Of Medical Entrance Exams
NEET EXAM :

  • NEET is a national-level entrance exam for admission to medical colleges.
  • Through NEET scores, students can secure admission in both state and central colleges.
  • Eligibility: Students can appear for NEET immediately after completing their 12th grade.
  • NEET facilitates direct MBBS admission 2024.

AIIMS EXAM :

  • AIIMS conducts a national-level entrance exam for MBBS admission.
  • Exam Dates: Scheduled to be held on 25th and 26th May 2024.

JIPMER :

  • Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education & Research conducts an entrance exam for MBBS
    admission.
  • Candidates must meet the eligibility criteria set by JIPMER for appearing in the entrance exam.
