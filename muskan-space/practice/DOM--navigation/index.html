HIB BONE DEFINATION

The hip bone is made up of three bones: the ilium, ischium, and pubis. These bones join to form the acetabulum, which is the socket of the hip joint. The hip joint is a ball-and-socket joint that connects the thigh bone (femur) to the pelvis.
